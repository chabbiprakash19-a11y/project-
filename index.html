ABOUT KITCHEN LOUNGE INC.

A Smarter Way to Buy Home Appliances.

The company brings multiple appliance and home solution categories together under one platform, helping customers compare options and make informed decisions.

With a focus on transparent pricing, product matching and single-point accountability, Kitchen Lounge works with homeowners as well as architects, interior designers, builders and project teams.
